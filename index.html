NYC Five Boroughs: Political Affiliation, Income, Education, and Race

This interactive map compares borough-level voter party enrollment with income, educational attainment, and racial/ethnic composition. In political affiliation mode, the map uses the selected party’s color scale: Democratic = blue, Republican = red, Conservative = purple, Working Families = green, unaffiliated/blank = gray, and other parties = gold. Boroughs are matched to counties: Bronx, Kings/Brooklyn, New York/Manhattan, Queens, and Richmond/Staten Island.
